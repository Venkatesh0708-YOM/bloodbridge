Become a BloodBridge Donor

Create your donor profile so people searching for blood can discover your availability.

Important Healthcare Notice: Registration does not confirm medical eligibility. Final donor eligibility and clinical blood compatibility are determined by qualified medical professionals at the time of donation.

1. Personal & Contact Information

Your basic details for account creation and urgent contact.

Please enter your full name.
Please enter a valid email address.
Used to reach you for urgent matching requests. Please enter a valid 10-digit phone number.
Donors must generally be at least 18 years of age. Please provide a valid date of birth (must be 18+ years old).

2. Blood Group & Donation History

Helps match your profile with compatible patient requests.

Please select your blood group.
You can change your availability at any time.
Leave blank if you have never donated before. Last donation date cannot be in the future.

3. Location & Travel Preferences

Privacy Notice: Exact residential street address is not requested.

Please enter your city or general locality.
