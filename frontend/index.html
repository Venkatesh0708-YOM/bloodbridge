Process Flow

How BloodBridge Works

A structured, transparent 4-step workflow connecting requests with available blood resources.

Step 01

Create a Request

Requesters submit required blood group, component type, needed units, hospital location, and urgency level.

Step 02

Find Potential Matches

BloodBridge calculates proximity, availability, and cooldown eligibility to identify relevant voluntary donors and blood banks.

Step 03

Connect

Donors and blood banks receive alerts and can respond or coordinate directly through structured platform channels.

Step 04

Medical Screening

Final donor suitability, compatibility testing, and transfusion decisions are handled strictly by certified healthcare professionals.

Platform Integrity

Built with Responsibility & Security

BloodBridge is engineered as a secure technology facilitator prioritizing privacy, clarity, and safety.

Secure Account-Based Access

Role-based access control with secure credentials ensures sensitive donor coordinates are never publicly exposed.

Structured Request Data

Standardized parameters eliminate ambiguity regarding exact blood components, units required, and hospital details.

Location-Aware Discovery

Proximity calculations discover matching candidates within a defined radius while preserving residential privacy.

Blood-Bank Resource Visibility

Enables registered facilities to maintain up-to-date component stock levels, reducing search times during emergencies.

Medical Decisions Remain with Qualified Professionals

BloodBridge is strictly a discovery facilitator. Donor medical eligibility, infectious disease screening, ABO/Rh cross-matching, and transfusion approvals must always be executed by certified blood-bank and healthcare clinicians.

Practical Prototype Notice: BloodBridge is being developed as a transparent prototype to demonstrate robust backend engineering, spatial discovery, and secure data modeling.
